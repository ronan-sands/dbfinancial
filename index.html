Finance Options

Choose a finance route that fits your plans.

Every purchase is different. DB Financial can help you consider suitable options for your asset, cash flow and business requirements.

HP

Hire Purchase

Spread the cost of an asset over an agreed term while working towards ownership. Ideal for machinery, vehicles and long-term business assets.

£

Leasing

Access the equipment or vehicle you need without paying the full purchase price upfront. A practical option for businesses that regularly update assets.

CH

Contract Hire

Use vehicles or equipment for a fixed period with predictable monthly payments. Suitable for convenience, budgeting and planned replacement cycles.

✓

Flexible Terms

Where available, payments can be structured around cash flow, seasonal income and the practical needs of your business.
